# MingOS Experience Constitution V1

> Status: **THOUGHT → implementation contract**  
> Date: 2026-08-27  
> Scope: `mingos.cn`, `ymai.love`, `ymai.fun`, `ymai.me`, and future MingOS-facing digital experiences.  
> Authority: **product/experience contract**. This document does **not** create MingOS Core primitives, Foundation hard invariants, clinical rules, or product runtime authority.

---

## 0. Why this document exists

MingOS is trying to build toward a place that does not yet exist.

Not a more impressive AI website.  
Not a louder claim about the future.  
Not a product that first creates anxiety and then sells relief.

The work begins from a quieter question:

> **当智能越来越靠近我们的生活，技术和一个人之间，还可以是什么关系？**

We do not need to prove that MingOS is powerful. We need to make a different relationship visible and real.

A person should be able to encounter a MingOS experience and notice, without being told repeatedly:

- the system may know more without owning more;
- the system may become stronger without making the person smaller;
- uncertainty can remain uncertainty;
- memory can remain contestable and removable;
- a family can contain multiple perspectives without being compressed into one profile;
- AI can help and then recede;
- a digital interaction can end by returning a person to real life.

This constitution exists so that those qualities survive changes in model, framework, designer, developer, product surface, and time.

---

# 1. Position

## 1.1 The center is not AI

The center is the person, the family, and the reality they are living.

MingOS interfaces are therefore **reality-centered**, not chat-centered.

The primary object on screen should usually be one of the following:

- what happened;
- what a person is experiencing;
- what different people saw differently;
- what is known and what is not known;
- what changed over time;
- what the person wants to keep, revise, refuse, or do next.

AI is a capability inside the space. It is not the owner of the space and does not need a persistent avatar, persona, or visual throne.

## 1.2 Stronger system, larger person

The long-term experience test is:

```text
system capability ↑
should imply
human clarity / agency / revisability / ability to act in reality ↑

not
hidden authority / dependency / engagement capture ↑
```

A successful interaction should leave the person **larger than before**: clearer, freer, more able to judge, refuse, choose, relate, or act.

## 1.3 The best interface ends in life

> **The best interface ends in life.**

A MingOS experience is not successful because a person stays longer.

Sometimes the right outcome is:

- record one thing and leave;
- understand one sentence and close the screen;
- stop analysing;
- go speak to someone;
- go outside;
- sleep;
- do nothing yet.

`No action` and `leave the interface` are legitimate product outcomes.

# 2. Brand posture

## 2.1 We do not manufacture the wound we promise to heal

> **Do not create the wound you promise to heal.**

MingOS must not rely on fear, shame, inadequacy, urgency, or catastrophe to create demand.

Avoid structures such as:

- “再不……就会……”;
- “AI 正在淘汰你”;
- “你的孩子正在被时代抛下”;
- “现代家庭已经失去连接”;
- “如果现在不改变……”;
- artificial countdowns, scarcity, panic metrics, or future-threat charts.

We do not need an enemy in order to exist.

AI can bring enormous value. Existing products can be useful. The world is not required to be broken for another possibility to be worth building.

## 2.2 We do not perform greatness

Avoid self-congratulatory positioning such as:

- revolutionary;
- world-first;
- redefines the future;
- next-generation life OS;
- unprecedented intelligence;
- industry-leading;
- “look how advanced our architecture is”.

If something is genuinely different, the experience should carry the evidence.

## 2.3 Presence over persuasion

MingOS does not need to win every visitor.

It should be possible to pass through a site without registering, buying, subscribing, or being chased.

The ideal residue is not “I was convinced”. It is:

> “原来技术还可以这样。”

# 3. Ten digital principles

These are experience defaults, not Core runtime primitives.

1. **Human before AI.** — 先有人，再有智能。
2. **Reality before interpretation.** — 先现实，再解释。
3. **Space before answer.** — 先留空间，再填答案。
4. **Clarity before intelligence.** — 清楚比炫耀聪明重要。
5. **Consent before memory.** — 先同意，再记住。
6. **Multiple perspectives before one profile.** — 先保留不同人的视角，不急着合成一种真相。
7. **Restraint before engagement.** — 克制比占据注意力重要。
8. **Revisable before certain.** — 允许修订，比假装确定重要。
9. **Life after interface.** — 数字交互最终回到现实。
10. **Leave the person larger.** — 离开系统时，人应比进入时更能成为自己。

When principles conflict, do not resolve by slogan. Return to current evidence, consent, safety authority, real user intent, and product context.

---

# 4. Attention ethics

MingOS does not optimize product value through compulsive attention capture.

## Do not default to

- infinite feeds;
- streaks;
- XP, levels, badges, leaderboards;
- red-dot pressure;
- “you are falling behind” reminders;
- dark patterns;
- unnecessary push notifications;
- forced continuation prompts;
- personalized anxiety;
- engagement loops that punish absence.

## Prefer

- finite surfaces;
- clear stopping points;
- explicit exits;
- quiet states;
- optional return;
- reminders only when materially useful and consented to;
- language that permits pause.

A good system must know not only how to respond, but **when not to continue**.

---

# 5. AI presence and disappearance

## 5.1 AI should not dominate the visual hierarchy

Do not make a permanent chatbot portrait, glowing agent orb, or anthropomorphic assistant the primary product identity.

The screen should foreground reality and the user’s own words.

## 5.2 Design the exit of AI

AI should recede when:

- the person has formed a judgement of their own;
- real-world action has begun;
- family members are directly communicating;
- the user is simply recording something;
- more analysis would only repeat;
- uncertainty is the honest endpoint.

A valid product state is **Quiet Mode**: the space remains available without prompting, interpreting, or demanding another message.

## 5.3 Do not over-anthropomorphize

Warmth is allowed. False personhood is not required.

Prefer:

- “我在这里。”
- “从你留下的这些记录里，我目前看到……”
- “这里我还看不清。”
- “可以先停在这里。”

Be cautious with:

- “我完全懂你。”
- “我一直陪着你。”
- “我替你感到……”
- emotional claims the system cannot literally possess.

# 6. Reality, evidence, and trust

## 6.1 Four visible trust levels

When materially relevant, distinguish:

### RECORD
What was actually recorded or explicitly reported.

> “8 月 17 日，你记录过……”

### PATTERN
A repeatable observation across records.

> “最近四次类似情况都发生在晚上。”

### INTERPRETATION
A possible explanation, clearly revisable.

> “一种可能是，最近持续疲劳在放大冲突。”

### SUGGESTION
An optional action candidate.

> “如果你愿意，今晚可以先不处理作业本身。”

Visual treatment must not make interpretation look more certain than record.

## 6.2 UNKNOWN is first-class

“I don’t know” is not a failure screen.

Use language such as:

> **这里我还看不清。**  
> 目前只有一次记录，我不想把它解释成长期模式。

Possible next actions:

- `先留着`
- `再告诉我一点`

Never manufacture certainty to keep a flow moving.

## 6.3 Revision is visible

If later evidence changes an earlier understanding, the product may show:

```text
此前理解
可能在回避学习

后来修订
主要压力可能来自同伴关系

为什么修改
出现了新的直接信息
```

Correction is not embarrassment to hide. It is evidence of a system that remains revisable.

# 7. Memory, consent, and boundaries

## 7.1 Memory must be inspectable

Where long-term memory is used, a person should be able to understand:

- what is remembered;
- why it was remembered;
- the source;
- whether it is record or interpretation;
- who can see it;
- how to correct it;
- how to remove it;
- what is intentionally not remembered.

## 7.2 Visible consent

Do not hide consequential memory behavior only in Settings or Terms.

For example:

> **这件事要留下来吗？**

- `只留在今天`
- `以后可以记得`
- `不要保存`

Exact wording is product-owned and context-dependent; the experience principle is that consequential consent is visible where the consequence occurs.

## 7.3 Shared and private are different spaces

In Family Space, a lock icon alone is insufficient.

Shared and private spaces should differ in layout, language, hierarchy, and data rules so users can feel where they are.

Private information must not leak through system-generated summaries, hints, or “helpful” cross-member references.

---

# 8. Multi-person reality

A family is not one account and not one truth profile.

The product must preserve the possibility that:

- a parent saw one thing;
- a child experienced another;
- both accounts are sincere;
- only some facts are jointly confirmed;
- some differences should remain unresolved.

A useful UI model may distinguish:

```text
发生了什么

妈妈这样看
爸爸这样看
孩子这样经历

目前能确认的
目前还不能确认的
```

This is a Family Space product pattern, not a MingOS Core schema requirement.

Do not silently compress multiple people into one diagnosis, score, or “family truth”.

# 9. Voice

## 9.1 Speak from concrete life

Internally, concepts such as “生命逻辑”, “主体性”, “生命基础设施” may be useful.

Externally, prefer the life a person actually recognizes:

- 一顿饭;
- 一个书包;
- 一次争吵;
- 一句话;
- 一个决定;
- 一段不知道怎么走的路;
- 一件过了一段时间才看懂的事.

The larger the idea, the smaller and more concrete the story should become.

## 9.2 Language characteristics

MingOS language is:

- calm;
- concrete;
- precise;
- non-coercive;
- capable of saying “不知道”;
- capable of stopping;
- warm without pretending intimacy;
- confident without performing certainty.

## 9.3 Avoid habitual product language

Use sparingly on brand surfaces:

- 赋能;
- 驱动;
- 全链路;
- 一站式;
- 重塑;
- 革新;
- 引领;
- 解决方案;
- 构建生态;
- “AI 深度分析”.

## 9.4 Chinese is a source language

Chinese is not a localization after English design.

`归`、`我们家`、`我的地方` carry meanings that should shape the interface directly.

English versions should preserve intent, not mechanically mirror syntax.

# 10. Visual language

## 10.1 Human Scale

The visual system should feel designed at the scale of a person, not a technology spectacle.

Primary materials:

1. **Typography** — the idea can stand without decoration.
2. **Time** — the interface allows pauses and sequences.
3. **Space** — whitespace is functional; not every question is immediately filled.
4. **Reality** — real traces of lived life enter the digital surface.

## 10.2 Open Form

Prefer forms that can remain open, revisable, and alive.

Do not turn every thought into a card, every relationship into a box, or every state into a closed score.

An open circle may be explored as a brand mark, not because it is a moon, but because it can mean:

- open;
- revisable;
- unfinished;
- alive.

No logo decision is finalized by this document.

## 10.3 Avoid generic AI aesthetics

Avoid by default:

- blue-purple AI gradients;
- glowing neural orbs;
- holographic brains;
- particle universes;
- glassmorphism for its own sake;
- dashboard-card walls;
- device mockups used only to signal “tech product”.

## 10.4 Color

V1 begins from neutral material rather than product-category color:

- Paper — warm off-white, not sterile white;
- Ink — near-black, not absolute black;
- Mist — quiet boundary tone;
- one restrained contextual accent when needed.

Four sites may develop distinct atmospheres, but not as “same template, four theme colors”.

## 10.5 Typography

Use modern sans-serif for operational clarity and restrained serif for selected narrative moments where Chinese reading benefits from it.

Production fonts must be legally distributable, performant in China and internationally, and self-hosted where appropriate. Do not ship font files into repositories without license verification.

# 11. Photography and illustration

## 11.1 Photography: reality, not performance

Three preferred categories:

### TRACE · 痕迹
The person is absent; life remains.

Examples: a school bag, an unfinished meal, a light left on, marked paper, shoes at the door.

### PRESENCE · 在场
People are living, not posing for the product.

### DISTANCE · 距离
The relationship between a person and the world: a road, bus window, hallway, city, doorway, sky.

Avoid stock-like scenes of smiling families doing homework, founders holding laptops, staged therapeutic intimacy, or heroic “young person on mountain” imagery.

Until authentic image material exists, **typography is better than fake authenticity**.

## 11.2 Illustration

Use illustration only when it clarifies structure.

Prefer sparse line diagrams between a working sketch and an engineering drawing.

Avoid decorative 3D, clay characters, mascot universes, and generic AI iconography.

# 12. Motion grammar

Motion has four primary semantic roles:

1. **Appear** — something becomes visible.
2. **Gather** — scattered evidence forms a tentative understanding.
3. **Unfold** — the person chooses to look deeper.
4. **Recede** — the system steps back and leaves the person’s words or reality in focus.

Motion should be slow enough to perceive, fast enough not to capture attention.

No animation exists only because the team knows how to build it.

`prefers-reduced-motion` must be respected.

# 13. Time character by site

The four sites share an ethical and visual family, but not one template.

## `mingos.cn` — slow time

A place to encounter the question and inspect what is being built.

## `ymai.love` — everyday time

Today, yesterday, recently, this year. Family life leaves traces over time.

## `ymai.fun` — present time

The emphasis is not “progress history” but what is happening now and whether the person can hear their own direction.

## `ymai.me` — lived time

An origin record: before AI, family work, questions, failed attempts, FamilyOS, MingOS, revisions, today.

# 14. Product-specific experience direction

## `mingos.cn`

Role: the front door to the MingOS world.

It should answer:

> “What relationship between human and intelligence are you trying to make real?”

It does not need to force registration.

## `ymai.love` · Family Space

Role: a place a real family can belong to.

Core external idea:

> **世界没有给一个家庭留一个地方。我们想留一个。**

The product should feel more like a home than an AI console.

## `ymai.fun` · 归 / Return to Oneself

Role: a place where the noise can quiet enough for a person to hear their own voice.

Core external idea:

> **世界有太多声音。这里，留给你自己的声音。**

No growth score, streak, rank, or gamified identity should become the default model of self-development.

## `ymai.me`

Role: origin record, not corporate “About”.

Core opening:

> **我不是从 AI 开始的。**

It should accumulate the real route: practice, questions, mistakes, revisions, artifacts, and why MingOS grew from them.

# 15. Performance and accessibility are craft

A quiet brand that loads slowly, excludes devices, or cannot be navigated accessibly contradicts itself.

Baseline target for production surfaces:

- WCAG 2.2 AA where applicable;
- keyboard-complete navigation;
- semantic HTML before ARIA patches;
- no information conveyed by color alone;
- touch targets appropriate for one-handed mobile use;
- scalable type;
- reduced-motion path;
- low-JS default where possible;
- no decorative dependency that blocks first meaningful content;
- image optimization and honest loading states.

Performance is respect for a person’s time, network, and device.

---

# 16. Evidence without performance

MingOS should show reality without turning evidence into theatre.

Public surfaces may distinguish:

- `EXISTS` — this is real now;
- `TESTING` — this exists but is under validation;
- `EXPERIMENT` — deliberately provisional;
- `DIRECTION` — a direction, not a delivered capability;
- `UNKNOWN` — we do not yet know.

Never turn a planned feature into a present-tense claim.

A useful public pattern:

```text
27 Aug 2026

What exists
...

What we are testing
...

What we do not know yet
...
```

Also make room for:

> **We were wrong about this.**

Explain what changed, what new evidence appeared, and what remains unknown.

---

# 17. One page, one primary action

Each page should have one dominant action unless reality requires otherwise.

Examples:

- `mingos.cn` — continue understanding / inspect what exists;
- `ymai.love` — enter the family space;
- `ymai.fun` — begin from now;
- `ymai.me` — continue reading the origin record.

Do not create a wall of competing CTAs simply because marketing conventions expect one.

---

# 18. The delete test

Before shipping a page, ask:

1. If 30% of this page disappears, is it stronger?
2. Does this card exist because the information needs a boundary, or because cards are easy to design?
3. Does this animation communicate meaning, or show capability?
4. Does the visitor need to understand this concept now?
5. Can the system carry this complexity so the person does not have to?

If an element has no clear reason to exist, remove it.

---

# 19. Release gates

A MingOS-facing experience does not become `REAL` because the page compiles.

Before release, pass six gates:

## G1 — STORY
Before the product is explained, can a person feel why this place exists?

## G2 — HUMAN
Does the page enlarge the person and reality, or enlarge AI and the company?

## G3 — RESTRAINT
What can be removed? What should the system deliberately not do?

## G4 — REALITY
Does the interaction return to real life, or trap value inside the screen?

## G5 — TRUST
Are record, interpretation, uncertainty, memory, permission, and visibility boundaries honest?

## G6 — CRAFT
Is typography, motion, responsive behavior, performance, accessibility, and failure-state quality at release standard?

Any failed gate blocks “finished” language.

---

# 20. Maturity markers

Use four experience maturity states:

### THOUGHT
An idea or direction. Not yet experienced.

### PROTOTYPE
Can be experienced, but not claimed as a production reality.

### REAL
Deployed and usable in its intended real environment.

### PROVEN
Real people have used it long enough, under defined conditions, to support the specific claim being made.

A status label is evidence discipline, not decoration.

---

# 21. Final test

When uncertain about a design, copy, interaction, feature, or animation, do not ask first:

> “Does it look like MingOS?”

Ask:

> **Does this make another relationship between human and intelligence more real?**

And then:

> **When this interface has done its job, is the person more able to return to life as themselves?**

If the answer is no, the design is not finished.
